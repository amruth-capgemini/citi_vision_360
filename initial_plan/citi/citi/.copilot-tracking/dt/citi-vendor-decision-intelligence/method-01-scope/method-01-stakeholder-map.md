## Primary Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role in the problem | Decisions supported | Validation focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| OCIO executive | Owns portfolio visibility, risk, and optimization outcomes | Renewal priority, concentration, and rationalization sponsorship | Which portfolio decisions are delayed or weakened today? |
| Vendor management lead | Operates supplier portfolio governance | Renew, compete, consolidate, remediate, or exit | What evidence takes the longest to assemble for each decision? |
| Sourcing and procurement | Owns commercial and sourcing analysis | Negotiation, sourcing events, and supplier consolidation | Which commercial context is missing or inaccessible at decision time? |
| Technology or product owner | Understands service and application dependencies | Change approval, operational-impact assessment, and alternatives | Which dependencies are material but currently invisible? |
| Finance analyst | Reconciles actuals, forecast, and variance | Forecast adjustment, variance investigation, and savings validation | What breaks the link between financial variance and operational context? |
| Third-party risk analyst | Assesses supplier and concentration risk | Escalation, remediation, monitoring, and control evidence | Which risk artifacts need controlled access or human review? |

## Enabling and Hidden Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Influence | Validation focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data steward | High | Entity resolution, semantic definitions, and data-quality remediation burden |
| Platform administrator | High | Access control, threshold configuration, source health, and segregation of duties |
| Information security, privacy, and legal teams | High | Entitlements, restricted evidence, acceptable use, and limitations of generated advice |
| Contract, ERP, TPRM, workforce, and CMDB system owners | High | Source availability, freshness, identifier quality, and integration constraints |
| Audit and compliance stakeholders | Medium | Traceability, explainability, policy checks, and decision-record expectations |

## Conversation Order Hypothesis

Start with the OCIO executive and vendor management lead to establish decision outcomes and urgency. Then validate daily evidence-assembly pain with sourcing, technology, finance, and risk analysts before checking feasibility with data, platform, and source-system owners.

## Open Questions

* Which stakeholder has final accountability when recommendation evidence conflicts?
* Who can approve a recommendation as actionable, and who can only view it?
* Which stakeholder groups are missing from the FSD but affected by a vendor change?
