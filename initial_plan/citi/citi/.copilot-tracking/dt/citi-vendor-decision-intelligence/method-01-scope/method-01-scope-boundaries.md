title: Method 1 Scope Boundaries
description: Initial problem-space boundaries based on the Citi Vendor Decision Intelligence FSD
---

## Original Request

Build a Vendor Decision Intelligence demo that provides a connected, governed Vendor 360 with evidence-backed answers, explainable recommendations, and non-destructive scenarios.

## Discovered Problem Hypothesis

Fragmented vendor, contract, spend, workforce, technology, performance, and ownership information forces decision-makers to manually assemble and reconcile evidence, delaying renewal, risk, rationalization, forecast, and scenario decisions.

## In Scope for Discovery

* The decisions that are currently delayed, error-prone, or difficult to evidence
* The workflow used to assemble decision evidence across commercial, workforce, technology, performance, and ownership data
* Trust needs for evidence, as-of dates, assumptions, confidence, entitlements, and traceability
* Stakeholder-specific needs for renewal, concentration, rationalization, variance, scenario, and executive Q&A decisions
* Constraints around data quality, entity identity, historical snapshots, access, risk thresholds, and scoring logic

## Out of Scope for the Initial Demo

* Executing renewals, purchase orders, workforce changes, or offboarding in systems of record
* Autonomous approval of commercial or risk decisions
* Replacing procurement, contract-lifecycle, TPRM, ERP, workforce, or CMDB systems
* Unapproved production integrations or sensitive production data
* Legal interpretation or final regulatory-compliance determination

## Frozen Constraints

* Recommendations must remain decision support and not become authoritative approval
* Access must respect role, row, attribute, and document-level entitlements
* Every material answer needs evidence and an as-of date
* Scenarios must not alter source data

## Questions to Validate

* Which decision has the highest business cost when evidence is slow, incomplete, or inconsistent?
* What manual work must be eliminated versus made more transparent?
* What does a stakeholder need to see before trusting a recommendation?
* Which scope boundary is fixed for the demo, and which could change after stakeholder conversations?
